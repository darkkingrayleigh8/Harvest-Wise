📚 Domain Research Citations & Data Provenance

NABCONS MoFPI Study (2020–22)

National annual post-harvest loss Rs 1.53 lakh crore. Fruit loss 6.02–15.05%, Vegetables 4.87–11.61%. Tomato total 11.62% (8.37% farm, 3.25% market). ICAR-CIPHET verified.

GIZ & MoFPI Processed Tomato Study (2016)

Processor procurement price target ~₹4.5/kg. Mandi crashes to ₹0.5–2/kg during gluts. Processing specs: TSS 5–6 °Brix, acidity ≥0.4%, pH <4.5, pulp recovery 40–50%.

Kolar APMC Volatility Study (2018)

Weekly price CV ~77%, arrivals CV ~102%, skewness 2.7. Bimodal arrivals with annual timing shifts. Justifies quantile forecasting over normal bell curves.

CEDA Agmarknet (Ashoka University)

Wholesale modal prices and daily arrivals schema. Demo data is deterministically synthesized based on real APMC parameters for the Pune region.
